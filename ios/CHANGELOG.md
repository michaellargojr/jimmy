# Jimmy version history

## 2.0 - Nov 1, 2026

The first version on Michael's iPhone, installed from Xcode with a free Apple ID.

- Backup to a folder in iCloud Drive after every finished workout, with Restore (Settings > Backup).
- Floating tab bar with the Workout tile in the middle.
- The Oct 19 and Oct 23 workouts from the old web app merged into his data.

## 1 - Oct 23 to Oct 31, 2026

The SwiftUI app built from Xcode and run in the Simulator on the Mac.

- Exercise library with bases, variations and styles.
- Set entry for weight, reps and activation, and the Workout card.
- Full history page and an always editable Overview.
- Move, copy or erase a variation's history.
- Rep range templates and the one-row Workout top bar.
- History sorted by muscle group or most recent.
- The flexing J app icon.

Before 1, Jimmy was a web app.
